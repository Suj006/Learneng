## Edits (exactly as requested)

| Card | Field | v2 | v3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| daughter | Meaning | A daughter is a girl child in a family. | **A daughter is a female child of a parent.** |
| daughter | Example | Meera is the only daughter in her home. | **Meera is Asha’s daughter.** |
| seat | Emoji | 💺 (vehicle seat) | **🪑** (chair, fits the living-room picture) |
| holiday | Example | In the summer holiday, we took a train to the hills. | **During the summer holidays,** we took a train to the hills. |
| straight | Spelling tip | silent 'gh' in the middle | silent 'gh' **near the end**: s-t-r-a-i-g-h-t |

**Holiday example:** "During the summer holidays." on its own is a sentence fragment, so the requested opening was used with the rest of the existing sentence.

**Not changed:**

- No synonyms, antonyms or word-family entries were added.
- No other meanings or bands changed.
- The daughter explanation was not edited (it wasn't requested). The v2 AI reviewer had called its first line vague: "A girl is a daughter in her home."
