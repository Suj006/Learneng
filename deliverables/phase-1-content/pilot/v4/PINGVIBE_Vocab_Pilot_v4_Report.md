> Edits applied to a copy of v3. **No API calls.** Not educator-approved, not published, not imported. "daughter" is **not** AI-approved.

The daughter card is now consistent with its meaning, "A daughter is a female child of a parent." All 40 cards pass every local check. Version 3 is unchanged; its checksums match.

## Changes (daughter only)

| Field | v3 | v4 |
|---|---|---|
| Explanation | A girl is a daughter in her home. Some girls live with two parents. Some live with one parent or with grandparents. | A girl is the daughter of her mother or father. She is still their daughter when she grows up. Families can be different. Some girls live with one parent and some with two. |
| Learning objective | …a daughter is a girl child in a family. | …a daughter is a female child of a parent. |
| Illustration brief | A smiling girl with her family. The family can be one parent, two parents or grandparents, shown warmly together. | A smiling girl with her parent or parents, shown warmly together. Show a one-parent or a two-parent family. |

**Why each change was made:**

- **Explanation:** the stale line has been replaced. The new wording ties "daughter" to her parent, so it matches the meaning, and keeps the point that families differ (one parent or two).
- **Learning objective:** it still stated the old "girl child in a family" meaning.
- **Illustration brief:** a girl shown only with grandparents would read as their granddaughter, not their daughter. The picture now always includes her parent or parents.

**Checked and kept** (no conflict with the meaning, no assumption about family structure):

- Meaning: "A daughter is a female child of a parent."
- Example: "Meera is Asha’s daughter." (kept as requested)
- Real-world sentence: "Riya is Amma's daughter."
- Collocations: only daughter, younger daughter, elder daughter.
- Word family: daughters, granddaughter.
- Related words: son, family, parent, granddaughter.
- Spelling tip, category, difficulty, grades, emoji.
