# PING VIBE — UI/UX, Audio, Navigation & Reward Issue Register (31 Oct 2026 cycle)

Method: code inspection plus a browser crawl and interaction scripts against a fresh **production build** (Chromium, Playwright), at 375 × 667 and 1366 × 768, with synthetic accounts. "Status" is updated only after the fix was re-tested. Results are in the final section and in `REGRESSION_TEST_REPORT.md`.

| ID | Priority | Area / page | Issue (observed) | Root cause | Fix | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| UX-001 | Critical | Sign-up: Birth month / year | On Windows (Chrome, Edge) the dropdown options look blank or missing | The `<select>` uses `.input`: **translucent white background and white text**. Windows draws the option list with the select's own colours over a white popup, so the options are white on near-white. The `<option>`s had no colours of their own | Opaque dark background and white text on `select` and `option` (`color-scheme: dark`), custom chevron, 48 px targets, placeholder shown in a muted colour || ✅ Fixed — verified at 320/375/768/1366: options render dark (#211B3F) with white text, select 52 px tall, value stays visible |
| UX-002 | High | Sign-up: Birth year | Year list offered ages 3–14, but PING VIBE is for 5–13; picking an edge year was accepted (age 3–4) or gave a vague error | Year range hard-coded in the form (`year-3 … year-14`); server allowed `thisYear-15 … thisYear-3`; month ignored | One shared, configurable age range (`AGE_MIN`/`AGE_MAX`, 5–13) used by the form, the server and the add-child form; exact age check using month and year; clear messages || ✅ Fixed — years 2021–2012 (ages 5–13); Dec 2021 rejected next to Year with the age message; month/year saved (export shows birth_month 6, birth_year 2018); AUTH-016…019 |
| UX-003 | Medium | Sign-up: Current grade | Requested as a dropdown; currently 10 small toggle buttons, which wrap unevenly on 320 px | Design choice | Child-friendly `select` like month and year, with the same styling and validation message || ✅ Fixed — grade dropdown, 10 grades, same style |
